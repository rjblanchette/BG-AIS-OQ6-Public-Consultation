## Consultation questions

1. Does the available evidence support R3-level systemic severity and scope, rather than only measurable individual persuasion or localized platform effects?
2. Are affected persons, communities, news institutions, or democratic institutions structurally unable to detect or resist the relevant aggregate distortions?
3. Are existing R2 controls, aggregate-risk governance mechanisms, and targeted R3 escalation adequate to contain the residual risk?
4. Would whole-domain R3 be proportionate, or is a narrower risk boundary better supported?
5. What evidence or argument weighs against R3 reclassification?
6. What future evidence would materially change your position?

## Privacy and provenance

GitHub issue comments are public. Do not post personal information that is unnecessary to your submission.

Authentic submissions may be entered into the OQ6-PP-01 participation register with a provenance reference back to the public comment. Personally identifying information is minimized to what is needed for provenance, declared affiliation/conflicts, and process integrity.

## How submissions are evaluated

This is not a referendum.

```text
submission count ⇏ classification authority
majority position ⇏ classification mandate
stakeholder prominence ⇏ evidentiary correctness
```

Arguments are assessed against evidence, counterevidence, BG-AIS risk criteria, proportionality, governance adequacy, and human decision autonomy.

## AI boundary

AI may assist with organizing and summarizing authentic submissions if provenance and disagreement are preserved. AI-generated stakeholder personas or fabricated submissions do not count as participation.

## Authority boundary

This public repository and its issue comments are participation/evidence surfaces only. They do not create BG-AIS governance authority and do not themselves determine R2 or R3.
