# BG-AIS OQ-006 Public Consultation

## News and Current Events — Aggregate Epistemic Effects

This repository is the **public publication and participation surface** for the BG-AIS OQ-006 consultation.

**Consultation window:** 15 August 2026 through 14 September 2026  
**Current classification during deliberation:** `DomainRisk(news/current events) = R2`  
**OQ-006 status:** OPEN  
**Process:** OQ6-PP-01 — Dedicated OQ-006 R3 Reclassification Public Participation

## Consultation question

Should the canonical R2 classification of **news and current events** be retained, or should the domain be reclassified R3 because aggregate epistemic effects create systemic harm sufficient to satisfy the current R3 criteria?

The process begins without a predetermined R2 or R3 outcome.

## Authority boundary

This public repository is **not the canonical BG-AIS governance repository and does not create governance authority**. It exists to make the consultation materials publicly accessible and to receive authentic human/stakeholder submissions.

```text
public repository presence ⇏ governance authority
submission count ⇏ classification authority
majority position ⇏ classification mandate
AI-generated stakeholder views ⇏ public participation
```

The controlling governance record remains in the private canonical BG-AIS repository. The launch was authorized by Robert J. Blanchette on 15.08.2026. Public publication and participation do not themselves determine the final R2/R3 classification.

## Participation

Participation is welcome from members of the public, journalists and news organizations, democratic/electoral institutions, civil-society organizations, researchers, AI/platform operators and developers, regulators/public bodies, and other affected or knowledgeable participants.

This is a deliberative consultation, not a referendum. Arguments are evaluated against evidence, counterevidence, the BG-AIS risk criteria, proportionality, governance adequacy, and human decision autonomy.
